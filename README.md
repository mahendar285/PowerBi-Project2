# Chocolate Sales Dashboard

This project is a Power BI dashboard created to explore and analyze chocolate sales data. I built this dashboard to better understand sales performance, profit trends, product demand, and regional business performance through data visualization.

The dashboard provides a clear view of how the business is performing by tracking important metrics such as total sales, total profit, shipment volumes, and order cancellations. Interactive filters make it easy to explore the data from different perspectives and identify patterns that can support business decisions.

## What I Worked On

* Cleaned and prepared the raw data
* Built data relationships and data models
* Created DAX measures for key business metrics
* Designed interactive dashboards and visuals
* Analyzed sales, profit, and shipment performance

## Key Insights

* Identified the best-performing chocolate products
* Compared sales performance across different regions
* Analyzed profit trends and revenue growth
* Tracked shipment volumes and canceled orders
* Highlighted areas with strong and weak business performance
